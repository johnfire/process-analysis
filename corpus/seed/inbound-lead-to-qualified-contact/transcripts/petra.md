**Petra Lindqvist:** Start with SAP: if it has enough stock for what was requested, confirm it. If not, use the supplier portal lead time. If neither gives you something you can stand behind, flag stock or lead time. Do not make a nice-sounding number up just because somebody wants the bid out.

**Interviewer:** What deadline are you always up against, and what makes it tight?

**Petra Lindqvist:** The worst version is confirming stock and then finding the count does not support it. Then the customer-facing bid has an availability that needs correcting. That is why I flag it if the information does not hold together; it is better than pretending.
