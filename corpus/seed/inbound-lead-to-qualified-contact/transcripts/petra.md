**Petra Lindqvist:** Don’t promise stock just because a number is sitting in SAP. If ERP stock covers the requested quantity, confirm it; if not, use the supplier portal lead time. And if neither gives you a clean answer, flag it. Do not invent a delivery date because somebody wants one.
